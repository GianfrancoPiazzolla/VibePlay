About VibePlay

VibePlay
GP
Gianfranco Piazzolla
Pushing the vibe coding era beyond!

Synopsis: VibePlay is a single-page music player that runs entirely in your browser. Load a local audio file, a whole folder, or a direct stream URL, give each one a reference name, and it becomes a reusable source in your personal library. Build a queue, reorder it by drag & drop, skip what you don't want, and keep listening with shuffle, repeat, bookmarks, resume points, a sleep timer and variable playback speed.
